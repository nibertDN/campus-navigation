About the application

Designed to simplify campus life for everyone.

Campus Navigation Assistant is a polished frontend dashboard that transforms how students, faculty, and campus visitors discover university spaces. The app combines interactive mapping, building intelligence, classroom schedules, QR-based attendance simulation, lost and found reporting, announcements, and safety contacts into a fast, accessible experience.

Our mission

To create a mobile-first campus companion that feels like an enterprise product without requiring backend services. Every interaction is powered by browser data storage and JSON-driven content so the experience is instantly available and easy to maintain.

  • Interactive navigation with map-based discovery
  • Search-first content for buildings, rooms, and schedules
  • Local Storage persistence for preferences, attendance, and reports
  • A modern, accessible, responsive UI designed for students.

Why it matters

Campus orientation can be overwhelming, especially during the first weeks of a semester. This assistant reduces friction by surfacing the most important resources in one clean interface.
